## [0.1.0-alpha.1] - 2026-08-04

First local prerelease baseline of the agent-native Windows editor. It includes the editor-independent document engine, authenticated localhost MCP, durable attributed traces and replay, illustration and indexed-pixel workflows, animated export, file/generation approval jobs, and native `.aidraw` persistence.

This version is not a v1 release. Advanced illustration, animation/map authoring, interchange fidelity, provider mock coverage, performance/accessibility gates, and public release evidence remain tracked work.
